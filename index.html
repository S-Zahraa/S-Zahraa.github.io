About

Zahra is a Biomedical Engineering graduate (University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore) working at the intersection of medical imaging and artificial intelligence. After research exposure at KIST, South Korea, she is now completing an associate degree in Computer Science, a 2 years program to deepen a technical foundation in computer science.

Current work centers on deep learning for medical image analysis: a completed low-dose CT denoising model (U-Net), an in-progress implementation of TransUNet for medical image segmentation, and a growing interest in multimodal AI for radiology.

Long-term goal: developing AI-driven diagnostic tools accessible to under-resourced hospitals, beginning with Pakistan.

MY MISSION
I believe AI's most meaningful contribution to medicine is not simply making good diagnosis better, but making reliable diagnosis possible where it is currently out of reach. My mission is to develop trustworthy medical AI that translates advances in research into meaningful clinical impact, particularly in the healthcare settings that need it most.
