About

S.Zahra is a Biomedical Engineering graduate (University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore) working at the intersection of medical imaging and artificial intelligence. After research exposure at KIST, South Korea, and now completing a second degree in Computer Science to deepen a technical foundation in AI and computer vision.

Current work centers on deep learning for medical image analysis: a completed low-dose CT denoising model (U-Net), an in-progress implementation of TransUNet for medical image segmentation, and a growing interest in multimodal AI for radiology.

Long-term goal: developing AI-driven diagnostic tools accessible to under-resourced hospitals, beginning with Pakistan.

Research Interests

Disease Detection & Diagnostics

Medical image analysis — segmentation, 3D reconstruction, image registration, multimodal imaging; computer-aided diagnosis and surgery.

Real-Time Applications in Healthcare

AI-assisted clinical interventions, including image-guided surgical assistance and ICU monitoring for early complication prediction.

Explainable & Multimodal Medical AI

Combining medical images with clinical text and other biomedical data for more reliable, interpretable diagnostic predictions.
